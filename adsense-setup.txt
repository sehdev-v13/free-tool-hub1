GOOGLE ADSENSE SETUP
1. Apply for Google AdSense using your own Google account and website.
2. After approval, replace ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX in index.html with your real publisher ID.
3. Add the AdSense script supplied by Google in the <head> of every page (or use your preferred site-wide integration).
4. Replace the visible Advertisement boxes with the exact ad-unit code generated in AdSense if you use manual ad units.
5. Keep Privacy, Contact and About pages accurate. Follow Google's current publisher policies and consent requirements applicable to your visitors.
NOTE: Google Ads is for buying advertising. Google AdSense is the product normally used to monetize a website by displaying ads.
